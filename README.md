## Sources

- `daily-audio-bible`: Free daily Scripture readings with commentary from [Daily Audio Bible](https://dailyaudiobible.com/); RSS via [FeedBurner](https://feeds.feedburner.com/dailyaudiobible).
- `daily-audio-bible-chronological`: The same daily readings ordered chronologically, from [Daily Audio Bible](https://dailyaudiobible.com/); RSS via [FeedBurner](https://feeds.feedburner.com/dailyaudiobiblechronological).
- `world-english-bible-podcast`: Public-domain audio of the World English Bible from [Spreaker](https://www.spreaker.com/show/7179091/episodes/feed).
- `sermonaudio-daily-sermon`: Daily devotional drawn from the [SermonAudio](https://www.sermonaudio.com/) sermon library; RSS at [sermonaudio.com](https://www.sermonaudio.com/rss_special.asp?type=daily).
- `renewing-your-mind-daily`: Daily Reformed teaching from [Ligonier Ministries](https://renewingyourmind.ligonier.org/); RSS at [ligonier.org](https://renewingyourmind.ligonier.org/rss).
- `solid-joys-daily-devotional`: A short daily devotional to feed your joy in Jesus, written by D. A. Carson, from [Desiring God](https://www.desiringgod.org/solid-joys); RSS at [feed.desiringgod.org](https://feed.desiringgod.org/solid-joys-audio.rss).
- `bibleproject`: In-depth conversations about the Bible and theology from [BibleProject](https://bibleproject.com/podcasts/); RSS at [Simplecast](https://feeds.simplecast.com/3NVmUWZO).
- `sermon-brainwave`: Weekly conversations with Luther Seminary faculty on the Revised Common Lectionary readings, from [Working Preacher](https://www.workingpreacher.org/); RSS at [Captivate](https://feeds.captivate.fm/sermon-brainwave-from-working-preacher/).
- `gospelbound`: Collin Hansen interviews guests about books, ideas, and the gospel in a post-Christian culture, for [The Gospel Coalition](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/podcasts/gospelbound/); RSS at [Simplecast](https://feeds.simplecast.com/FSKZVjpz).
- `pastors-talk`: A weekly conversation between Jonathan Leeman and Mark Dever about practical Christian life and pastoral ministry, from [9Marks](https://www.9marks.org/podcast/pastors-talk/); RSS at [Transistor](https://feeds.transistor.fm/pastors-talk-a-podcast-by-9marks).
- `word-on-fire-show`: Bishop Robert Barron on the Catholic faith and culture, from [Word on Fire](https://wordonfire.org/); RSS at [Libsyn](https://rss.libsyn.com/shows/71279/destinations/302449.xml).
- `pints-with-aquinas`: Matt Fradd on Catholic doctrine and culture, from [Pints With Aquinas](https://pintswithaquinas.com/); RSS at [Megaphone](https://feeds.megaphone.fm/BVDWV3128780668).
- `daily-orthodox-scriptures`: A year-long reading through the Bible with brief commentary, from [Ancient Faith](https://www.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/dailyscriptures); RSS at [feeds.ancientfaith.com](https://feeds.ancientfaith.com/DailyOrthodoxScriptures/).
- `lord-of-spirits`: Fr. Andrew Stephen Damick and Fr. Stephen De Young on the Orthodox understanding of the seen and unseen world, from [Ancient Faith](https://www.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/lordofspirits); RSS at [feeds.ancientfaith.com](https://feeds.ancientfaith.com/TheLordOfSpirits/).
- `kmtt-torah-podcast`: Daily Torah lectures from Yeshivat Har Etzion in Jerusalem, from [KMTT](https://kmtt.libsyn.com/); RSS at [Libsyn](https://rss.libsyn.com/shows/18464/destinations/12657.xml).
- `just-thinking`: Real questions about faith, science, and God answered by Darrell Harrison and Virgil Walker, from [Ravi Zacharias International Ministries](https://justthinking.me/); RSS at [Anchor](https://anchor.fm/s/3463c2f0/podcast/rss).
- `apologetics-live`: Listener questions and apologetics teaching with Andrew Rappaport, from [Striving for Eternity](https://strivingforeternity.org/); RSS at [Captivate](https://feeds.captivate.fm/apologetics-live/).
- `in-the-arena`: The debates and lectures of Dr. William Lane Craig on God and the resurrection, from [Reasonable Faith](https://www.reasonablefaith.org/); RSS at [Blubrry](https://feeds.blubrry.com/feeds/inthearena.xml).
- `contending-for-truth`: Dr. Scott Johnson defending the Christian faith with Scripture and logic, from [Contending for Truth](https://www.contendingfortruth.com/); RSS at [contendingfortruth.com](https://www.contendingfortruth.com/feed/podcast).
- `jimmy-akin-podcast`: Jimmy Akin on Catholic apologetics, from [Catholic Answers](https://www.catholic.com/tjap); RSS at [catholic.com](https://www.catholic.com/audio/tjap/podcast).
